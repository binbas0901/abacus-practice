    Listening

    Record family voices. Each person records the same 33 short words once (about 3 minutes); the app builds every number from them. Pick a voice above, or "Take turns" once two or more people have finished.

    Technique checklist (watch for these)

    • Thumb pushes 1-beads up; index finger pushes them down and moves the 5-bead both ways.
    • Work left to right on multi-digit numbers; one fixed movement per case, never counting beads one by one.
    • Small friends (make 5): 1&4, 2&3. Have them say the partner out loud before moving.
    • Big friends (make 10): 1&9, 2&8, 3&7, 4&6, 5&5. "+7 is +10, take 3."
    • The beads are the authority: read the answer off the abacus, don't compute in the head and then set it.
    • Short daily sessions beat long weekly ones. Stop while they still have energy.

    Backup

    Progress, settings and recorded voices are kept on this device only. Save a backup file now and then (Files → iCloud Drive works well) so nothing is lost if the iPad is reset or replaced. You can restore it here or on another device.

    Sound check

    No sound? Check the iPad volume and that the side switch or Control Centre is not on silent, then tap Test sound.
