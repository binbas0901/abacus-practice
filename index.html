Technique checklist (watch for these)

  • Thumb pushes earth beads up; index finger pushes them down and moves the five-bead both ways.
  • Work left to right on multi-digit numbers; one fixed movement per case, never counting beads one by one.
  • Small friends (5-family): 1&4, 2&3. Have her say the partner out loud ("4 needs 1") before moving.
  • Big friends (10-family): 1&9, 2&8, 3&7, 4&6, 5&5. "+7 is +10, take 3."
  • The beads are the authority: she should read the answer off the abacus, not compute in her head and then set it.
  • Short daily sessions beat long weekly ones. Stop while she still has energy.
